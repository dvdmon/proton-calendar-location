### Working on an Issue

Before beginning substantial work on an open issue, please leave a comment expressing your interest and briefly describing your proposed approach.

This helps coordinate contributions and avoid duplicated effort.

A project maintainer will confirm whether the issue is available and can assign it to you where appropriate.

When submitting a pull request, reference the related issue so other contributors can see that work is underway.

Please note that issue assignments do not guarantee that a proposed change will be merged. All contributions are subject to review.
